ASTRO RACING

Sixteen destinations. Twelve racers. Three laps of shortcuts, drifts, and flying coconuts.

Launch gameBack to Arcade

Pick your race

Race solo against Easy, Normal, or Hard bots. Choose a driver, kart or bike, wheels, and colors in the garage.

Desktop controls

WASD or arrows to drive · Space to drift · Q for an item · E for an air trick · C to look back · R to recover · Esc to pause.

Phones and friends

Hold your phone comfortably in landscape, tap Enable Tilt, grant motion permission, and hold still until Tilt ready. Hold the large Drive button on the right to accelerate; release to coast. Drift is on the left; Item and Trick are above Drive. Brake / reverse stops, then backs up. Tip an edge down to steer toward it; return to your centered grip to go straight. Recenter is at the top. After rotating or returning from another app, hold steady and resume. Auto-accelerate is optional and starts off. If tilt is unavailable, choose Touch steering: hold an arrow, or hold Drift and slide sideways.

Online rooms are not available yet. Solo racing is ready to play.
