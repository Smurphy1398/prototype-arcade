What changed since the last published candidate (v3.1.19):

  • Performance repairs: measured 16.7 ms median / 16.8 ms p95 frame time on both maps during a walk (headed browser, real GPU, 1584×805). Not a heavy-combat benchmark.

Known, disclosed limitations:

  • Zombie window breach: zombies break boards (slowly) but pop through a cleared opening with no climb animation yet.
  • Mall upper floor: zombies do not yet follow you up the escalators.
  • Boards: build/pry can return more boards than were spent.
  • Mall combat frame rate under many enemies is not benchmarked; 60 fps there is a target, not a verified claim. First glass crack / first gunshot may hitch once.
  • Mall shops are sparse and loot is not yet store-appropriate; mall railings are opaque; audio contention (MP5/M4A1) still open.
