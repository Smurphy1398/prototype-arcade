Rubik’s Cube Trainer

A Rubik’s Cube that teaches you to solve it, then times you like a competition. Twenty-six real cubies in CSS 3D, a step-by-step guided solve, a WCA-standard timer with inspection and Ao5/Ao12, and a 2-look OLL/PLL drill — in one file, with no libraries.

Launch Game Back to Arcade

Build Info

Version: v2.0.0 — guided solve + timer
Status: Complete prototype
Platform: Browser / HTML — no libraries, no build step, no network

Four modes

  • Play — free orbit, camera lock, snap views, undo
  • Guide — learn the layer-by-layer method, one explained move at a time
  • Timer — 15s inspection, +2 / DNF, Ao5 / Ao12, per-stage splits
  • Train — drill 2-look OLL and PLL recognition

Controls

  • Drag to orbit; drag a sticker to turn its layer
  • Keys U D L R F B turn a face, Shift reverses
  • Space drives the timer; 0 recenters, C locks the camera
  • Speed mode adds the two-hand competition grip

Known Notes

  • Solutions follow the beginner method, so they are long (~100–200 moves) by design — a computer-optimal solver would be shorter and would teach nothing
  • Outer quarter turns only: no slice or wide moves, so Z-perm is absent rather than approximated
  • The first layer is yellow rather than white, because the centres are fixed and white sits on top. The method is identical — only the colour differs

Runs entirely offline — no CDN, no network, no packages. Works on desktop and phone.
