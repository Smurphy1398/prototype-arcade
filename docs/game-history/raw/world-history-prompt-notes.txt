Ai prototype - world history map

Here are my notes for a project, what do you think, can you use html to create this;

Interactive map of world history, zoom in/out and navigate history with a year slider starting from earth's evolution to modern day; including but not limited to the evolution of human and civilization, Pangaea to the biblical days, continents, to the ancient civilizations, to the biblical days, BC to AD, from the printing press to modern day information technology, to the modern day map, show the shifts in territories and countries through the years. This should be an informative and interactive experience, a way to seamlessly and chronologically explore history, and map it all together.

Include pictures of important historical figures, landmarks, civilizations, militaries, inventions, etc.

It should be organized and structured so it's easy to understand on a surface level but allows you to dive deeper. It would contain a lot of information.

I think there could be in addition to this freeroam map experience there can be actually guided experiences that can automatically take you through a tour of the history of certain regions to automatically exploring certain regions, continents, civilizations and empires, and major wars. Maybe include the option to narrate the tours with subtitles and text to speech(option to disable tts, volume controls, speed, voice type) also make it say the name of any selected region and a button in the summary list to read it to you.

This should look and feel good, I won't go in depth with features but I'm hoping you can get the idea from my notes and can correctly provide what I'm looking for, the information needs to be factual, maybe with citations/links to sources, I think Wikipedia might be the best bet, but I trust you on this project
