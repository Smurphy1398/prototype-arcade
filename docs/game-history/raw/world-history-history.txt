Historical era framework corrected and expanded:
Prehistoric Time: c. 30,000–3500 BCE
Early Historic Civilizations: c. 3500–800 BCE
Carthage / Phoenician Mediterranean: c. 814–146 BCE
Roman Kingdom: 753–509 BCE
Roman Republic: 509–27 BCE
Roman Empire: 27 BCE–476 CE in the West
Late Antiquity: c. 200–750 CE
Early Middle Ages: c. 500–1000 CE
High Middle Ages: c. 1000–1300 CE
Late Middle Ages: c. 1300–1500 CE
Renaissance: c. 1350–1600 CE
Early Modern World: c. 1500–1800 CE
Modern Industrial Age: c. 1800–1945
Postwar / Cold War: 1945–1991
21st Century / Information Age: 1991–present

Corrections from your notes that I built in:

Rome's traditional founding date is usually 753 BCE, not 750 BCE.
The Roman Republic is usually dated 509–27 BCE, not 510–31 BCE.
The Roman imperial period normally begins with Augustus in 27 BCE, not 31 BCE.
Late Antiquity overlaps the later Roman Empire instead of coming cleanly after it.
The Renaissance is often dated broadly from around 1350–1600, while the printing press around 1450 and Columbus in 1492 happen inside/near the broader Renaissance and early modern transition.
